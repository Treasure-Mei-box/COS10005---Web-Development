ORCHID Restaurant

Ajooma Pocha

Korean

If you cannot decide what to eat in the vibrant SS15 area, Ajooma Pocha is one of its most underrated authentic Korean eateries. Run by a friendly Korean lady, the small pojangmacha-style restaurant serves genuine Korean comfort food, from bubbling budae jigae to fresh kimbap, while prices stay well within a student budget. Portions are generous, so it is best for simple dates and small gatherings.
