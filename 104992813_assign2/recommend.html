How the matching works

  • Cuisine category: choose a specific cuisine, or pick "Any" to see all types. A restaurant must match your chosen category.
  • Diet: a restaurant must cater for your dietary preference.
  • Budget: restaurants whose average spend sits inside your range score highest; those that only partly overlap score lower; those outside it are left out.
  • Purpose: restaurants that suit family, date or business dining earn extra points.
