Photo Slideshow of My Student Life

The slideshow below brings together nine photographs from my own collection. It moves from my daily routine in Malaysia to the places I travel to. Each photo fades into the next one automatically, and the filled dot under the frame follows the photo that is currently on screen. The animation is made with CSS only.

My Original Student Video

The video below is 80% my own recording and editing with some videos that my friend took of me. It uses the HTML5 <video> element, so it plays inside the page without leaving the browser.

How This Page Is Arranged

The top half of the page is a photo slideshow built with HTML and CSS only. The @keyframes rule fades each photograph in and out, and animation-delay decides how long each photo stays on screen.

The bottom half is the video player. The video file is edited using the short videos that I have taken randomly recorded myself and some videos that my friend took of me.

Use the gallery further down the page if you want to see every photograph on its own with its full caption.
