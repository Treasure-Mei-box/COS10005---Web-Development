Activities I Enjoy as a Student

I prefer activities that are affordable, easy to reach and possible to enjoy. The following activities fit into my student life in Malaysia without requiring a large budget.

  • Meeting friends at a café or affordable restaurant
  • Walking, jogging or doing light exercise at a nearby park
  • Visiting exhibitions or local cultural events for window shopping
  • Taking photographs and exploring different parts of city
  • Watching films, reading or relaxing at home
  • Visiting public parks, markets and shopping areas in my suburb

My Typical Daily Study Routine

I study for Bcs.Data Science at INTI college,Subang in Malaysia. The table below shows how I organise a typical study day. I will add my university name and adjust the times if they are different from my real routine.

Example of my typical daily study routine
Time Activity What I Do
8:00–9:00 Travel and preparation Travel to university and prepare the materials I need for the day.
9:00–11:00 Classes Attend lectures or practical classes and take notes.
11:00–12:00 Review notes Review what I learned and ask questions about difficult topics.
12:00–1:00 Lunch and break Have lunch with friends and take a short break.
1:00–3:00 Assignment work Work on coding, written tasks or group activities.
3:00–4:00 Independent study Practise programming or revise class material.
4:00–5:00 Plan and relax Check unfinished tasks, plan the next session and relax for the evening.

My Study Tip

I study in smaller blocks rather than trying to complete everything at once. I set a goal for each session and take short breaks. This helps me stay focused and stop feeling overwhelmed.
